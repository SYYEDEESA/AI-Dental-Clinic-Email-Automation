# General Enquiry / FAQ Workflow

1. Classify the email as `enquiry`.
2. Send the message to the FAQ AI Agent.
3. Generate a concise response using verified clinic knowledge.
4. Email the response to the sender.
5. Log the interaction in Google Sheets.

## Safety

- No diagnosis or prescriptions.
- No invented prices, policies, services, or availability.
- Unknown answers must be escalated to clinic staff.
