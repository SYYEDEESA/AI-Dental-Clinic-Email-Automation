# Job Enquiry Workflow

1. Classify the email as `job_query`.
2. Send an acknowledgement to the applicant.
3. Store the enquiry or application in Google Sheets.
4. Notify clinic staff.

## Recommended Stored Fields

- Sender name
- Sender email
- Position of interest
- Phone number
- Experience
- Qualifications
- Resume attachment status
- Original email
- Received timestamp
